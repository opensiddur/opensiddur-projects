## Humash (annual cycle)

The whole humash for the annual cycle: Miqra al pi ha-Masorah facing the JPS 1917 translation, with the annual haftarah of each parsha (Ashkenazi rite). No date is declared, so every festival reading and megillah is kept, and the triennial Torah divisions appear as markers in the margin.

## Ha-Siddur ha-Shalem (Birnbaum, 1949)

Birnbaum's siddur, Hebrew facing English, with no date or place declared. Every day-dependent passage (the rain seasons, the Ten Days, Ya'aleh v'Yavo, Anenu) is kept with its rubric, as in the printed book; a variant for a particular day would declare it.
